# North Star — v1.1 (signed by the owner, 2026-10-02; v1.1 adds the owner's "2D with heavy 3D" clarification)

Every agent's output is judged against this page. It changes only with a new version number. Where it conflicts
with `owner-voice.md`, the owner's words win.

## Purpose

derprito64bit's portfolio: **one person, one story, two doors.**

- The **Front Door** (`/`, the 2D site) tells the whole story to anyone, on any hardware, in minutes.
- **The Manor** (`/manor/`, the 3D Unity world) is the "they built *this*?" layer for desktop visitors. It is a
  candlelit royal home whose collection includes paintings you can walk into.

## Audience

Recruiters, mentors, teammates and competition judges. Most are not gamers. Most give it 3–10 minutes, often on a
work laptop or a phone.

## The four beats (everything must serve at least one)

| Beat | Deadline | The visitor knows… |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Identity | 10 s | who derprito64bit is and what they make |
| 2. Best work | 60 s | the strongest projects, *shown*, not described |
| 3. Everything | 3 min | every project, award and medal, and how to reach the owner |
| 4. Craft | 10 min (desktop) | the owner builds worlds: the Manor, the camera, the Painting Worlds |

## What each door owns

- **Front Door:**
  - beats 1–3 completely, plus beat 4 as a teaser and the "Enter the Manor" handoff;
  - **a 2D site with heavy 3D.** The layout, reading order and navigation are 2D and editorial. Real three.js 3D
    (models, scenes, mesmerising effects and transitions) is a core ingredient, not decoration. The owner named
    two references: portfolio-zxc (3D models used heavily) and Jesper Landberg (3D-driven effects in a 2D site).
    On weak hardware the 3D degrades to designed stills and posters, never to a broken page;
  - it is the SEO and sharing surface;
  - phones only ever see this door.
- **Manor:**
  - beats 3–4 as a place:
    - the Grand Gallery (projects);
    - the Hall of Honours (statue-scale awards and medals);
    - the Camera Room (lenses, film looks);
    - the Painting Wing (walk-in worlds, with *The Starry Night* first);
    - the Arcade;
  - skippable at every point: the Grand Gallery is always one step away.

## One identity, two media

- **Shared (invariant):**
  - the name and monogram;
  - the voice: first person, plain, specific and confident, never hype;
  - the content: one source, `content/`;
  - **one accent colour**;
  - **one bridging typeface**;
  - **one spring table** for motion constants.
- **Different on purpose:**
  - The Front Door has its own look, set in the direction tournament. It is not candlelit and not royal.
  - The Manor keeps the candlelit royal home.
  - Each medium does what only it can.

## Is / is not (the owner's picks)

- **It is:** **crafted · playful · tactile**
  - Every detail is made by hand.
  - Surprises reward curiosity.
  - Things feel physical: springy, weighty, touchable.
- **It is not:** **templated · corporate · loud**
  - No stock layouts or card grids.
  - No sterile business tone.
  - No visual shouting.

Every design decision is checked against these six words.

## Non-negotiables

1. **No invented facts.** `content/owner-facts.md` is the only fact source; everything else is a visibly marked placeholder.
2. **Free tools only:** no paid tiers, watermarks or licences that bar a personal portfolio.
3. **A weak-hardware floor:** the Front Door is complete and beautiful with WebGL off, on a 5-year-old laptop and a mid-range phone.
4. **WCAG 2.2 AA**, with reduced motion fully respected.
5. **Budgets:**
   - Manor: ≤120 batches, ≤60k triangles per zone, ≤8 Ultra-only lights.
   - Front Door: page budgets as listed in `budgets.md`.
6. **Slop:**
   - Nothing generic. No templates, no unearned cards or gradients, no decoration without a beat.
   - Banned copy: seamless, elevate, immersive journey, unleash, cutting-edge, revolutionize, next-level, world-class.
